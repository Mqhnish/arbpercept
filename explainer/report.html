How the check works

1. Facts onlyThe log line becomes a small set of facts. The writer gets nothing else, not even the time of day.
2. Every number, checkedEach number in the answer must match a fact, rounding allowed. A skipped trade can't be called sent.
3. Fail means retryA bad answer goes back with the problem named. After two misses the plain template is shown instead.
